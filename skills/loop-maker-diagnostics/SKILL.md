# Loop Maker Diagnostics

Diagnose the workflow and its evidence, not just code or agent infrastructure.

1. Describe the observable symptom and its impact.
2. Separate confirmed facts from hypotheses.
3. Classify the failure:
   - Goal/input ambiguity or contradiction.
   - Weak research, stale sources or insufficient evidence.
   - Reasoning, calculation or analysis defect.
   - Output quality, format, tone or schema mismatch.
   - Planning assumptions, dependency or feasibility failure.
   - Software/data validation failure.
   - Agent, provider, connector or tool failure.
   - State conflict, interruption or context truncation.
   - Resource exhaustion, timeout or evaluation disagreement.
   - Security, privacy or authorization issue.
4. Identify the smallest test that can distinguish likely causes.
5. Change one material factor at a time where practical.
6. Verify the recovery against the original acceptance criteria.
7. Record signal, cause (confirmed or suspected), action, evidence and remaining uncertainty.

Never relabel a hypothesis as a fact. Do not repeat a failed attempt unchanged. Stop on security or authorization violations.
